Our Story

The best Chinese food
in the Hamptons.

Dragon Garden has been serving the Southampton community with authentic Hunan, Szechuan, and Cantonese cuisine for years. We pride ourselves on using 100% vegetable oil and fresh, quality ingredients in every dish.

Whether you're craving the bold heat of a Szechuan classic, the comforting flavors of Cantonese roast pork, or a hearty American-Chinese staple, our kitchen has you covered. Large portions, fair prices, friendly service.
